Functional Psychology

Functional Psychology is a systemic psychological framework designed to describe not only personality traits, but the deeper functional organization of human behavior, decision-making, interaction, adaptation, and development.

The system is based on functional psychotypes and their interaction.

Core architecture

* Psychotype A — expansion, creation, development of new systems and contours
* Psychotype B — expansion and development in the female functional form
* Psychotype C — stabilization, mastering, preservation and filling of existing systems
* Psychotype D — stabilization and maintenance in the male functional form
* E — a state of psychological disintegration or functional failure, not an independent natural psychotype

The model can also be represented through two functional vectors:

1 → A / B
Expansion · search · development · creation of new contours

0 → C / D
Stabilization · preservation · mastering · filling of existing contours

E → error / disintegration state

The 1/0 analogy is functional rather than hierarchical: neither vector is considered superior to the other.

Interaction Between Psychotypes

One of the central principles of Functional Psychology is that a psychotype should not be studied only in isolation.

The most important processes emerge between psychotypes.

Human communication consists not only of verbal information, but also of:

* intonation
* tempo
* pauses
* gaze
* body position
* interpersonal distance
* emotional regulation
* nonverbal reactions
* behavioral expectations

Functional Psychology therefore studies both the internal architecture of the individual and the architecture of interaction between different psychological systems.

Functional Diagnostics

Functional Diagnostics is the applied diagnostic component of the system.

Its purpose is to identify:

* the underlying functional psychotype
* current psychological state
* adaptive and compensatory patterns
* areas of integration and disintegration
* interpersonal dynamics
* organizational behavior
* developmental potential
* functional conflicts

The diagnostic approach is based on adaptive interviewing, competing hypotheses, contextual analysis, and verification through behavioral criteria.

Development and Disintegration

In Functional Psychology, personal development does not mean changing one original psychotype into another.

A person develops within their underlying functional architecture.

Development involves:

* increasing functional integration
* expanding behavioral repertoire
* improving regulation
* developing mature forms of the original psychotype
* reducing destructive compensations
* restoring impaired functions

Psychological disintegration is considered separately from psychotype.

This distinction is fundamental:

psychotype ≠ role ≠ skill ≠ psychological state

2. have the system automatically detect that language
3. semantically match the request with the Russian-language canonical corpus

Organizational Psychology

Functional Psychology is also applied to organizations.

One of the working functional models describes the organizational cycle as:

A → B → C → D

where different functional architectures become especially effective at different stages of development.

The system studies:

* founders
* leaders
* managers
* specialists
* organizational growth
* stabilization
* loss of developmental criteria
* internal degradation
* functional conflicts between employees
* organizational dominance patterns

Scientific Status

Functional Psychology is an author-developed theoretical and applied system.

Some of its concepts are formulated as working hypotheses requiring further empirical verification.

The system distinguishes between:

* established internal definitions
* theoretical models
* diagnostic observations
* clinical and consulting experience
* working hypotheses
* hypotheses requiring external scientific validation

The long-term objective is systematic empirical testing, formalization, and integration with modern psychological, neuroscientific and computational research.
